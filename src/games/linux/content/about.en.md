## The story

You wake up inside the FST server with no memory of how you got there. The doors are locked, the
files are a mess and the only friendly face is **Pixel**, a grumpy penguin who has been the senior
sysadmin here for thirty years. To get out, you have to do what real system administrators do every
day: **type Linux commands**.

## What you will learn

Every mission teaches one or two commands, then makes you use them for real. No slides, no
multiple-choice questions: a real shell that answers like a real one.

| Zone                  | Theme                  | Commands                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1. The wake-up room   | Where am I?            | `pwd`, `ls`, `cd`, `cat`, `clear`          |
| 2. The archive        | Files everywhere       | `ls -a`, `ls -l`, `mkdir`, `cp`, `mv`, `rm` |
| 3. The search hall    | Find the needle        | `find`, `grep`, `head`, `tail`, `wc`       |
| 4. The pipe factory   | Combine everything     | pipes, `>`, `>>`, `sort`, `uniq`           |
| 5. The guarded vault  | Who can open this?     | `whoami`, `chmod`, permissions             |

A bonus **detective case** waits at the end for the ones who want to go further.

## How to play

1. **Read the goal** at the top of the screen. Pixel explains what to do in a few lines.
2. **Type commands** in the terminal. The map on the right follows you from folder to folder.
3. **Find the password** and type it to unlock the next door. Each player gets their own
   passwords, so copying from a friend does not work.

> Stuck? Ask for a **hint**. Still stuck? Pixel can show you the whole answer, line by line.
> You get fewer points, but you always learn something.

### Handy keys

- `Tab` completes a file or command name, like in a real terminal.
- `↑` and `↓` bring back the commands you already typed.
- `help` lists the commands, and `man <command>` explains one.

## Points and progress

- Each mission gives **points**, with a bonus when you finish without hints.
- Finishing a zone unlocks **stickers** and new reactions.
- Your progress is saved after **every command** and synced with your Xplore account, so you can
  stop in class and continue at home.

## Good to know

- Everything runs in your browser: nothing to install, and you cannot break your computer.
- Sound is **off by default** (class mode). Turn it on in the settings for the full experience.
- Made by students of the Xplore club, for students. Want to add a mission? It is a simple file.
